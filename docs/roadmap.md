# Implementation roadmap

1. Raw ingestion and Bronze publication.
2. Statute/decree and FAQ-specific Silver parsers.
3. Legal cross-reference resolution and temporal change detection.
4. Customer Reference QA schema, profiling, and privacy controls.
5. Claim-evidence lineage inference benchmarked against a human-labeled subset.
6. Retrieval Blueprint compilation and version-aware cache.
7. Budgeted question and answer generation.
8. Deterministic quality gates, LLM judge calibration, and human review.
9. Impact-based rebuild and immutable release publication.

Each milestone requires a contract, quality gate, lineage record, cost record, and test.
